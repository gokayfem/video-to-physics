## 1. Governing model
2-D incompressible Navier–Stokes in the dye plane z = 0.013 m (ζ = z/h = 0.5):
∂u/∂t + (u·∇)u = −∇p/ρ + ν∇²u − λu, ∇·u = 0; passive tracers ∂c_k/∂t + u·∇c_k = κ∇²c_k (κ → numerical only).

**Why 2-D:** Re = 150 < Re_A ≈ 190 (mode-A onset, Williamson 1996 ARFM 28:477; Barkley & Henderson 1996 JFM 322:215 give 188.5),
so the unbounded wake is 2-D laminar periodic. **Limits (model-conditioned, declared):** the real layer is shallow, h/D = 2,
with a no-slip floor and free surface. The cylinder's floor junction produces a horseshoe vortex and the shedding is not exactly
parallel along the 20 mm span (end effects, oblique/cellular shedding). Low-aspect-ratio cylinders typically shed at a few percent lower St.
The 2-D model ignores this, and the mismatch is not quantified from data here. The vertical velocity profile is represented only
through the drag term.

**Floor friction.** For a laminar layer (no-slip floor, stress-free surface) the developed profile is the half-Poiseuille u(ζ) = 1.5ū(2ζ−ζ²),
which gives u_mid/ū = 1.125. The depth-averaged bottom friction is λ_bulk = 3ν/h² = 7.5e-3 s⁻¹. The slowest vertical mode (cos(πζ/2)-type)
decays at λ_mode = (π²/4)ν/h² = 6.17e-3 s⁻¹. That mode governs how fast mid-depth vortices lose circulation, so the model uses λ_mode.
- Shallow-wake stability number (Chen & Jirka 1995, JFM 291:323; Ingram & Chu 1987): S = c_f D/(2h) = **0.0056**, with c_f = 2λ_bulk h/ū = 0.0225.
  The critical value S_c ≈ 0.2–0.5, so S/S_c ≈ 0.03 and the vortex street is **not** suppressed. The expected St shift is < 1 %.
- Not negligible for amplitudes: λD/U = 0.0041, which is 0.62 × the viscous rate 1/Re at the cylinder scale. Vortex circulation
  decays by e^(−λt) = 0.94 over 10 s and by about 0.92 during transit through the visible 17 D. The drag also makes sidewall layers
  saturate at the Brinkman length √(ν/λ) = 12.7 mm instead of growing without limit, which is the real shallow-channel behaviour.
  **Decision: add it.** It costs one source term. E checks a λ = 0 twin run and expects |ΔSt| < 1 % and a lower Cl′ or vortex amplitude downstream.

**Blockage** β = D/W = 1/12. Use an effective-velocity correction: U_eff = U(1+ε) (Allen & Vincenti 1944, NACA Rep. 782; Maskell 1963, ARC R&M 3400),
with solid blockage ε_s = (π²/12)β² = 0.0057 (doublet images) and wake blockage ε_w = C_d β/4 = 0.0275, so ε = 0.0332.
Corrected targets: St_b = St_W(Re(1+ε))·(1+ε), Cd_b = Cd_∞(1+ε)², Cl′_b = Cl′_∞(1+ε)².
The sidewall layers (δ* ≈ 10 mm at x_cyl) make the effective width ≈ 0.10 m (β_eff ≈ 0.1). This and the correction's own accuracy
give about ±1.5 % on St and ±3 % on Cd, which is why the production tolerances are wider than the clean benchmark tolerances (§6).

## 2. Boundary conditions and domain
Domain x ∈ [−0.30, 0.30], y ∈ [−0.06, 0.06]; no-slip on y = ±0.06 and the cylinder (D = 0.01 at (−0.04, 0)).
**Inflow:** a real header (diffuser, honeycomb, screens) delivers a nearly uniform lateral profile with thin wall layers. The vertical
profile then develops downstream. Over 0.26 m to the cylinder, the floor layer grows to δ99 ≈ 5√(νx/U) ≈ 21 mm ≈ h, so at the cylinder
the vertical profile is close to the developed half-parabola. That justifies both u_mid/ū = 1.125 and the λ drag.
Laterally, plug inflow develops sidewall layers. Parabolized marching (derive.py) gives a centreline speed-up of 1.109 at x_cyl with drag
(1.120 without), so **U_in = 0.01352 m/s** (λ = 0 variant: 0.01339). A fully developed 2-D Poiseuille inflow would be wrong:
the development length U W²/ν is about 200 m. See panel (c) of the visual.
**Outflow:** zero-gradient, p = 0, 34 D downstream of the cylinder. Vortices must leave without reflection, so check that the
probe signal at x = 0.13 is unaffected when the domain is lengthened by 0.1 m (optional).

## 3. Developed periodic state at t = 0
D/U = 0.667 s. With an impulsive start and only round-off noise (amplitude about 1e-10, global-mode growth rate σ ≈ 0.12 U/D at Re 150),
saturation takes about 230 D/U ≈ 155 s. **Seed** an asymmetric perturbation instead, for example a cylinder rotation pulse or
v = 1e-3 U in the near wake. Then growth takes about 60 D/U and amplitude settling about 40 D/U. Base-flow development needs
two flow-throughs (2 × 40 s = 120 s = 2 × 60 D/U).
**Precursor ≥ 120 s (180 D/U).** Periodicity criterion over the last 5 periods: |ΔCl′|/Cl′ < 1 %, |ΔSt|/St < 0.5 %, |ΔCd_mean| < 0.5 %.
**Phase anchoring:** define phase by Cl(t) ∝ sin(2πf t + φ₀). Choose the start of the 10 s window (t = 0) inside the last precursor
period so that φ₀ matches A's measurement (which side rolls up first in the reference's first ~1.5 s). This is the only free choice.
Record φ₀ and its source in meta.json.

## 4. Dye
Passive, Sc = ν/D_m ≈ 1000. Molecular spreading √(2D_m t) ≈ 0.17 mm over the full transit, which is negligible.
Visible streak width (0.5–1 mm) is set by the needle and the wake, so **numerical diffusion must stay below it**.
Required: Δ ≤ 0.125 mm in tracer regions (≥ 4 cells across a 0.5 mm streak; Basilisk level 12 on 0.6 m = 0.146 mm is marginal, so use 13
adaptive on tracer gradients, or level 12 plus a limited BCG scheme with the dye-width convergence check in §6).
**Sources:** Dirichlet c = 1 in a disk of r = 0.35 mm (needle OD/2) at x = −0.093, y ∈ {0, ±8, ±16, ±24 mm}. Each colour group
[0,1,2,0,1,2,0] is one tracer, normalised to 1 at the needle, so source strength does not matter.
**Release timing:** potential-flow travel from the needle to x_front = x_cyl + 0.6D = −0.034 m takes 3.87 s (±8 mm), 3.88 s (±16 mm)
and 3.90 s (±24 mm). Release all needles at **t_rel = −3.87 s**. At t = 0 the fronts sit at −0.0340…−0.0343 m. The y = 0 streak
stagnates on the cylinder face and splits around it.

## 6. Benchmarks (benchmarks.json → benchmarks)
- **Schäfer & Turek 1996 2D-2** (Notes Num. Fluid Mech. 52:547): channel 2.2 × 0.41, D = 0.1 at (0.2, 0.2), ν = 1e-3, parabolic inflow
  with U_max = 1.5 (Ū = 1), Re = 100, λ = 0. Pass if St ∈ [0.2950, 0.3050], Cd_max ∈ [3.22, 3.24], Cl_max ∈ [0.99, 1.01]
  (Δp at t₀ + T/2 ∈ [2.46, 2.50] optional). Coefficients use Ū and D.
- **Unbounded** (width ≥ 40 D, uniform inflow, λ = 0): Re 100 → St 0.164 (Williamson) / 0.165, Cd 1.33, Cl′ 0.33 (Park, Kwon & Choi 1998,
  KSME Int. J. 12:1200; Henderson 1995, Phys. Fluids 7:2102: Cd 1.35). Re 150 → St 0.1834, Cd 1.32–1.33, Cl′ 0.52
  (Inoue & Hatakeyama 2002, JFM 471:285; Henderson 1995). **Tolerance: St ≤ 2 %, Cd ≤ 3 %.** This is the clean physics check.
  The blockage-corrected production targets (§5) carry the wider 3 %/5 % tolerances.
- **Hopf onset** Re_c ≈ 46–47.5 (Provansal, Mathis & Boyer 1987, JFM 182:1; Jackson 1987, JFM 182:23). Control: Re 40 decays to a steady
  symmetric wake; Re 55 sheds.
- **Convergence:** 3 levels, ratio 2 (finest D/Δ ≥ 64). Observed order p ≥ 1 (expected ≈ 2). Fine-grid GCI: St ≤ 1 %, Cd ≤ 2 %, Cl′ ≤ 3 %.
  Dye: L1(level 12 vs 13) < 0.5 × L1(level 11 vs 12) at frames 0, 120, 240.
- **Divergence:** max|∇·u|·D/U ≤ 1e-3, rms ≤ 1e-5. **Tracer:** mass balance |Σc dV − (injected − outflow)|/injected ≤ 1 %;
  bounds −1e-3 ≤ c ≤ 1.001. **Energy:** no injection. After the precursor, domain KE is stationary (drift < 1 % per 10 s).
